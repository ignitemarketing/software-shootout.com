The method

How we actually do this.

Every shootout starts in production. Not a vendor demo, not a sandbox — a real implementation, with real users, doing real work.

We score on twelve dimensions: time-to-value, total cost over three years, configurability, customisability, mobile-app quality, reporting, support response, ecosystem depth, security, performance, contract terms, and the human factor.

We disclose every relationship. If a vendor paid us, you’ll see it on the page. If we resell the product, you’ll see that too. No undisclosed affiliate links. Ever.
